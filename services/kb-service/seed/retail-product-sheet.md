# Retail Product Sheet

## Savings Plus

Savings Plus is a personal savings account. The interest rate on Savings Plus is 3.10 percent a year on the whole
balance, calculated daily and paid monthly on the last business day of the month. The minimum balance to open
Savings Plus is 5,000. There is no monthly fee on Savings Plus while the balance stays at or above 5,000; below that
the monthly fee is 4.95. Two withdrawals a month are free; each further withdrawal costs 2.00. Savings Plus is CDIC
insured up to 100,000 per depositor.

## Money Market

Money Market is a tiered savings account. The interest rate on Money Market is 3.65 percent a year on the first
100,000 and 3.90 percent on the balance above 100,000. The minimum balance to open Money Market is 25,000. There is
no monthly fee on Money Market. Withdrawals are unlimited, with 48 hours' notice for a withdrawal above 50,000.
Interest is calculated daily and paid monthly. Money Market is CDIC insured up to 100,000 per depositor.

## Everyday Chequing

Everyday Chequing is the personal transaction account. The monthly fee on Everyday Chequing is 11.95, waived with a
minimum balance of 3,000 or a recurring payroll deposit. Debit transactions and Interac e-Transfers are unlimited and
free. One paper statement a month is free; each extra statement costs 2.50. The overdraft limit is 500, with interest
on the overdrawn balance at 21 percent a year. A book of 50 personal cheques costs 45.00.

## Choosing between Savings Plus and Money Market

Savings Plus suits a customer saving for a goal within two years who wants a low entry balance. Money Market suits a
customer holding 25,000 or more who wants the higher tiered rate and unlimited access. A customer with between
5,000 and 25,000 can only open Savings Plus. Both accounts can be opened in branch in ten minutes with one piece of
government photo identification.
