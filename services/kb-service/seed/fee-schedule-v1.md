# Fee Schedule

## Transfers and payments

An outgoing domestic wire transfer costs 30.00. An outgoing international wire transfer costs 45.00. An incoming wire
costs 15.00. A stop payment on a cheque costs 12.50. A certified cheque costs 10.00. A bank draft costs 9.95.

## Account service fees

A non-sufficient funds item costs 45.00. An overdraft transfer costs 5.00 per transfer. A paper statement beyond the
free monthly one costs 2.50. A returned item costs 7.00. An account closed within 90 days of opening costs 20.00.

## Card fees

A replacement debit card costs 5.00. A foreign ATM withdrawal costs 3.00 plus the foreign network's charge. Foreign
currency transactions carry a 2.5 percent conversion fee.
