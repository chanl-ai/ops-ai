# Branch Operations Manual

## Customer identification

Every new account needs one piece of valid government photo identification and one proof of address dated within
the last 90 days. A passport, a driver's licence or a provincial photo card is accepted. Expired identification is
never accepted. Record the identification type and number on the account opening form.

## ATM and debit limits

The daily ATM withdrawal limit on a personal debit card is 1,000. The daily point of sale limit is 3,000. A branch
manager can raise either limit for one day up to double the standard limit when the customer presents photo
identification. Interac e-Transfer is limited to 3,000 a day and 10,000 a week.

## Stop payment procedure

To place a stop payment on a cheque, confirm the customer's identity, then record the cheque number, the amount,
the payee and the date written. Enter the stop payment in the core banking system under Account Services. The fee is
charged to the account at entry. A stop payment stays in force for six months. Tell the customer that a cheque
already cleared cannot be stopped.

## Wire transfers

Outgoing wires received before 3 pm local time are sent the same business day; after 3 pm they are sent the next
business day. A wire above 10,000 needs a second teller's verification. Wires above 100,000 need branch manager
approval. International wires need the beneficiary bank's SWIFT code and the purpose of payment.

## Holds on deposited cheques

A cheque deposited in branch is held for four business days; a cheque deposited at an ATM is held for five. A
customer with an account open for more than 90 days may have the first 100 of a deposit released at once.
