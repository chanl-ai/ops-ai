# Card dispute time limits

Owner: Card Services. Regulated document.

A customer must raise a card dispute within 120 days of the statement date that first shows the charge. Disputes raised later are declined unless the customer was unable to raise them, for example because of hospitalisation.

Credit card disputes: the disputed amount is credited provisionally within 10 business days while the dispute is investigated.

Debit card disputes: the customer is not responsible for losses after reporting a lost or stolen card, or for transactions they did not authorise, if they protected their PIN.

Investigation time: most disputes close within 45 days. Disputes with a merchant abroad can take up to 90 days.

What staff record: the date the customer raised it, the transaction, the reason in the customer's words, and whether the card was blocked.
