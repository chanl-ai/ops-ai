# Chargeback reason codes

Owner: Card Services. For dispute staff.

| Group | Reason | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Fraud | Card not present, not authorised | The customer did not make an online or phone purchase |
| Fraud | Card present, counterfeit | A copy of the card was used in person |
| Authorisation | No authorisation | The merchant charged without an approval |
| Processing | Duplicate charge | The same purchase was charged twice |
| Processing | Incorrect amount | The amount charged differs from the receipt |
| Consumer | Goods not received | Paid-for goods or services never arrived |
| Consumer | Cancelled recurring charge | A subscription was charged after the customer cancelled it |
| Consumer | Not as described | Goods were materially different from what was sold |

Evidence needed: receipts, the cancellation confirmation, or messages with the merchant. Consumer disputes need proof the customer first tried to resolve it with the merchant.
