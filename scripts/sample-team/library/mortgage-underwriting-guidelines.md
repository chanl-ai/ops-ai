# Mortgage underwriting guidelines

Owner: Residential Lending. Policy document.

## Qualifying rate
Applicants qualify at the higher of the contract rate plus 2 percentage points, or the minimum qualifying rate set by the regulator.

## Debt ratios
- Gross debt service ratio at most 39%.
- Total debt service ratio at most 44%.

## Down payment
- At least 5% on the first $500,000 of the price and 10% on the portion above it.
- Under 20% down needs mortgage default insurance.
- Down payment comes from the applicant's own savings, a gift from immediate family with a signed gift letter, or the sale of a property.

## Income documents
Salaried applicants give a recent pay stub and an employment letter. Self-employed applicants give two years of tax assessments.

## Property
An appraisal is needed when the loan is over 80% of the value or the property is rural.
