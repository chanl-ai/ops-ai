# Wealth fee disclosure

Owner: Wealth Management. Regulated document.

## Managed accounts
| Assets under management | Annual fee |
|---|---|
| First $250,000 | 1.50% |
| Next $750,000 | 1.25% |
| Above $1,000,000 | 0.95% |

Fees are charged quarterly in arrears, based on the average month-end value.

## Self-directed accounts
- Stock and ETF trades: $9.95 each.
- Annual account fee of $100, waived above $25,000 in assets.

## Fund fees
Mutual funds carry management expense ratios between 0.25% and 2.10%, shown in each fund's facts document.

Every client receives an annual report of the fees and charges they paid, in dollars.
