# Branch operations manual

Owner: Branch Operations. For branch staff only.

## Opening the branch
1. Two staff members open the branch together; one is a supervisor.
2. Disarm the alarm within 60 seconds and record the time in the opening log.
3. Check the ATM vestibule and night deposit box before unlocking the public doors.

## Teller limits
- A teller drawer holds at most $5,000 in cash at any time; excess cash goes to the vault in a sealed bag.
- Cash withdrawals over $10,000 need a supervisor's approval and a large cash transaction report.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit for a customer card is $1,000 unless the customer has a raised limit.

## Identification at the counter
A customer withdrawing cash at the counter shows their bank card and PIN, or one piece of government photo ID.

## Closing the branch
Tellers balance their drawers before the supervisor closes the vault. A drawer difference over $50 is reported to the branch manager the same day.
