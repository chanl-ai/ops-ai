# Customer identification program

Owner: Financial Crime Compliance. Regulated document.

## Accepted identification
- Passport, driver's licence or provincial photo ID card, valid and not expired.
- Or two of: a utility bill, a credit file record, a bank statement, a government benefits letter, each from a different source.

## Beneficial owners
For a business, identify every person who owns or controls 25% or more, and confirm the business exists with its registry record.

## Politically exposed persons
Ask every new customer whether they or a close family member hold a senior public role. A yes needs senior management approval within 30 days.

## Updating information
Refresh identification for high-risk customers every year and for others when something material changes.
