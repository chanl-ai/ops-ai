# Small business credit policy

Owner: Commercial Credit. Policy document.

## Who can borrow
Businesses operating for at least two years with annual revenue between $100,000 and $5 million.

## Lending limits
| Product | Maximum |
|---|---|
| Unsecured line of credit | $100,000 |
| Secured line of credit | $500,000 |
| Equipment term loan | $1,000,000 over at most 7 years |

## Credit tests
- Debt service coverage ratio of at least 1.25 on the last two years' financial statements.
- Owners with 25% or more of the business give a personal guarantee.
- A personal credit score of at least 650 for each guarantor.

## Approval authority
Requests up to $250,000 are approved by a senior account manager. Larger requests go to the regional credit committee.

## Annual review
Every facility is reviewed each year with updated financial statements.
