# Complaints handling policy

Owner: Customer Experience. Policy document.

## Stage 1: front line
Staff acknowledge a complaint the same day and try to resolve it within 5 business days. Every complaint is logged in the complaints system, even when resolved on the spot.

## Stage 2: complaints office
If the customer is not satisfied, the complaint goes to the complaints office, which sends a written final answer within 56 days of the complaint being first made.

## External ombudsman
If the customer is still not satisfied, or 56 days pass without a final answer, the customer may take the complaint to the external banking ombudsman. Staff give the ombudsman's contact details in the final letter.

## Vulnerable customers
Complaints involving a vulnerable customer are flagged and handled by a senior specialist.
