# Home equity line questions

## How much can I borrow?
Up to 65% of your home's value as a revolving line, and up to 80% combined with your mortgage.

## What is the interest rate?
The prime rate plus 0.50%. Interest is charged only on what you use.

## What are the minimum payments?
Interest only each month. You can repay the principal at any time without a charge.

## How long does approval take?
Usually 5 to 10 business days, including an appraisal of your home.

## Are there fees?
Legal and appraisal fees of up to $1,000 are covered by the bank on lines of $50,000 or more.
