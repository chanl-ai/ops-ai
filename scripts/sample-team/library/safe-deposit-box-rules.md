# Safe deposit box rules

Owner: Branch Operations.

Box sizes and yearly rent: small $60, medium $110, large $210. Rent is charged each year on the rental date.

Access: the renter or a named co-renter signs the access card and shows photo ID. Staff never see or handle the contents.

Keys: the renter holds two keys. A lost key costs $50 to replace; losing both means the lock is drilled at the renter's cost of $250.

Unpaid rent: after 90 days unpaid, the bank writes to the renter. After one year unpaid, the box may be drilled in front of two witnesses and the contents listed and held.

Prohibited items: cash over $10,000 is not recommended, and weapons, hazardous materials and perishable goods are not allowed.
