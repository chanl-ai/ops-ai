# Lost or stolen card

## What to do right away
Lock your card in the mobile app under Card controls, then call the 24-hour card line. Locking stops new purchases at once; you can unlock it if you find the card.

## Are you responsible for the charges?
You are not responsible for transactions made after you report the card lost or stolen. For credit cards, zero liability applies to unauthorised purchases when you protected your PIN and card.

## Getting a new card
A replacement card arrives by mail within 5 to 7 business days. An emergency card can be sent within 2 business days, or picked up at a branch the next business day.

## Recurring payments
Your new card has a new number. Update any subscriptions and automatic bill payments that use the old one.
