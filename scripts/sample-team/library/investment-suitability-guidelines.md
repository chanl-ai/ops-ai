# Investment suitability guidelines

Owner: Wealth Compliance. Regulated document.

## Know your client
Before recommending an investment, record the client's goals, time horizon, risk tolerance, risk capacity, income, net worth and investment knowledge.

## Suitability test
An investment is suitable only if it fits the client's risk profile and puts the client's interest first. Concentration in one security over 10% of the portfolio needs a documented reason.

## Updates
Review the know-your-client information at least every 36 months, and every 12 months for managed accounts, or after a material change.

## Leveraged investing
Borrowing to invest is suitable only for clients with high risk tolerance, stable income and net worth above $250,000, and needs a supervisor's sign-off.
