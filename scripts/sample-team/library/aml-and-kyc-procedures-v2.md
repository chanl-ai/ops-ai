# AML and KYC procedures

2026 revision. Owner: Financial Crime Compliance. Regulated document. Replaces the 2025 edition.

## Identifying a customer
Verify identity with one piece of government photo ID checked by a person or by the approved digital ID tool, or two documents from different reliable sources, before opening an account.

## Large cash transactions
Report any cash transaction of $10,000 or more, or several in 24 hours that add up to $10,000, within 15 days.

## Large virtual currency transactions
New in 2026: report virtual currency received of $10,000 or more within 5 working days.

## Suspicious transactions
Any employee who suspects money laundering escalates it to the AML desk the same day. Never tell the customer that a report is being considered.

## Record keeping
Keep identification and transaction records for at least five years after the account closes.

## Training
Every employee completes AML training within 30 days of joining (was 60) and every year after.
