# Personal loan rate card

Owner: Retail Lending. Rates effective 1 October 2026.

| Credit tier | Secured loan | Unsecured loan |
|---|---|---|
| Excellent (760+) | 7.49% | 9.99% |
| Good (700 to 759) | 8.49% | 11.99% |
| Fair (650 to 699) | 10.49% | 14.99% |

Loan amounts: $5,000 to $50,000. Terms: 1 to 5 years.

Prepayment: customers can repay all or part of a personal loan at any time with no charge.

Rate discount: 0.25 points off for customers with automatic payments from a chequing account at the bank.
