# Retail product sheet

Effective 1 September 2026. Owner: Retail Products.

## Everyday chequing
- Monthly fee: $4.95, waived with a minimum daily balance of $3,000 or one recurring deposit of $500 or more each month.
- Unlimited debit transactions in Canada; Interac e-Transfers included.
- Overdraft protection available on request (see Overdraft protection terms).

## Premium chequing
- Monthly fee: $16.95, waived with a minimum daily balance of $5,000.
- Includes two free non-network ATM withdrawals per month and one free standard draft per month.
- Annual fee rebate of up to $139 on the Travel rewards card.

## High-interest savings
- Interest calculated daily on the full balance and paid monthly. Current rates are on the Deposit rate sheet.
- Two free withdrawals per month; each further withdrawal costs $5.00.

## Youth and student accounts
- No monthly fee for customers under 19, or full-time students under 25 with proof of enrolment.
- Student accounts convert to Everyday chequing at age 25, with 60 days' notice to the customer.

## What staff must tell the customer
Staff must give the customer the fee schedule before opening any account and record that it was given.
