# Outgoing wire transfer procedure

Owner: Payments Operations. Control document.

## Before sending
1. Verify the customer's identity in person or by a recorded callback to the phone number on file.
2. Confirm the beneficiary name, account number and bank code with the customer, reading them back.
3. Check the payment against the sanctions screening tool; a possible match stops the wire.

## Approval limits
| Amount | Approval |
|---|---|
| Up to $25,000 | Branch supervisor |
| $25,000 to $100,000 | Branch manager |
| Over $100,000 | Branch manager and Payments Operations |

## Cut-off times
Domestic wires received by 3 p.m. Eastern are sent the same day. International wires received by 1 p.m. Eastern are sent the same day.

## Fees
The fee schedule sets the fee. Correspondent bank charges on international wires are passed to the customer unless they choose to pay all charges up front.
