# Privacy notice

## What we collect
Your name, contact details, identification, account and transaction history, and how you use our website and app.

## Why we use it
To open and run your accounts, to meet legal duties such as preventing money laundering, to protect you from fraud, and, if you agree, to tell you about products.

## Who we share it with
Credit bureaus, payment networks, service providers bound by contract, and regulators or law enforcement when the law requires. We never sell your information.

## Your choices
You can withdraw consent to marketing at any time in the app or by calling us. Withdrawing does not affect your accounts.

## How long we keep it
At least seven years after your last account closes, or longer when the law requires.

## Contact
Write to the Privacy Office to see or correct your information. We answer within 30 days.
