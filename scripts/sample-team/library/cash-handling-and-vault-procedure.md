# Cash handling and vault procedure

Owner: Branch Operations. Control document: any change needs Operational Risk review.

## Dual control
The vault is opened and closed by two staff members, each holding one half of the combination. No one person may hold both halves.

## Vault limits
- The branch vault holds at most $250,000 in cash. Cash above that is shipped to the cash centre on the next armoured car run.
- Cash orders are placed by 2 p.m. the business day before delivery.

## Counting and sealing
- Cash is counted twice, by two people, before it is sealed in a tamper-evident bag.
- The bag number, amount and both counters' names are recorded in the vault ledger.

## Differences
A vault difference of any amount is reported to the branch manager at once and to Operational Risk within 24 hours.
