# RRSP and TFSA contribution rules

## RRSP
- You can contribute 18% of last year's earned income, up to the yearly maximum, plus any unused room.
- Contributions made in the first 60 days of the year can count for the previous tax year.
- Over-contributions above a $2,000 buffer are taxed at 1% a month.

## TFSA
- The yearly limit for 2026 is $7,000. Unused room carries forward.
- Withdrawals are added back to your room on 1 January of the next year, not right away.
- Over-contributions are taxed at 1% a month on the highest excess.
