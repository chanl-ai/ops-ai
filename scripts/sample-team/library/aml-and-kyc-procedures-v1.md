# AML and KYC procedures

2025 edition. Owner: Financial Crime Compliance. Regulated document.

## Identifying a customer
Verify identity with one piece of government photo ID, or two documents from different reliable sources, before opening an account.

## Large cash transactions
Report any cash transaction of $10,000 or more, or several in 24 hours that add up to $10,000, within 15 days.

## Suspicious transactions
Any employee who suspects money laundering escalates it to the AML desk the same day. The AML desk files a suspicious transaction report as soon as practicable once reasonable grounds are found.

## Record keeping
Keep identification and transaction records for at least five years.

## Training
Every employee completes AML training within 60 days of joining and every year after.
