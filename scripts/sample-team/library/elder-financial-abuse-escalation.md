# Elder financial abuse escalation

Owner: Customer Protection. Control document.

## Warning signs
- A new person suddenly managing an older customer's banking.
- Large or unusual withdrawals, or a customer who seems confused or fearful.
- Changes to a power of attorney or beneficiaries that the customer cannot explain.

## What staff do
1. Speak with the customer alone where possible.
2. Delay a suspicious transaction for up to 2 business days, with the branch manager's approval.
3. Escalate to the Customer Protection team the same day using the vulnerable customer form.

## Trusted contact
If the customer has named a trusted contact person, Customer Protection may contact them. Without a trusted contact, the team may contact adult protective services.

Never accuse a family member in front of the customer.
