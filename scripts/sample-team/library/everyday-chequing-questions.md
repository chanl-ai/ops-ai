# Everyday chequing questions

## How much is the monthly fee?
The Everyday chequing account costs $4.95 a month. The fee is waived in any month you keep at least $3,000 in the account every day, or receive a recurring deposit of $500 or more.

## How many transactions are included?
Unlimited debit purchases, bill payments and Interac e-Transfers in Canada.

## Can I get paper statements?
Yes, for $2.00 a month. Online statements are free and kept for seven years.

## How do I order cheques?
Order them in online banking under Account services, or at any branch. The first book of 25 cheques is free.

## What happens if I go overdrawn?
Without overdraft protection, a payment that would overdraw the account may be returned and a $45.00 returned item fee charged. See Overdraft protection terms to avoid this.
