# Overdraft protection terms

Owner: Retail Products. Regulated document.

## Who can have it
Overdraft protection is available on Everyday and Premium chequing accounts for customers aged 18 or over who pass a credit check. The limit is between $100 and $5,000.

## How it works
- A payment that takes the balance below zero is paid up to the overdraft limit.
- Each item paid into overdraft costs an overdraft handling fee of $5.00, at most $20.00 a day.
- Interest of 21% per year is charged daily on the overdrawn balance and taken monthly.

## Pay-as-you-go option
Customers without a set limit may choose pay-as-you-go: each item paid into overdraft costs $5.00, and items over $100 may be returned.

## Ending overdraft protection
The customer can end it at any time in branch or by phone once the account is back above zero. The bank gives 30 days' written notice before reducing or ending a limit, unless the account is in default.
